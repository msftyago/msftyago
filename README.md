## about
Hello visitor! Let me introduce myself, I am a Network Engineer with a strong background in programming, someone who can handle variety of things. And by “variety,” I really mean variety. Growing up around computers, i mastered a lot of skills. Maybe my strict upbringing helped, or i just had many good reasons for studying things. And yes, i can fix printers or install Windows. If you ask me, though, i'd install you Linux and teach you all about it, i consider being liberal in sharing knowledge the highest act of human connection.
